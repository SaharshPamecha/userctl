Built a CLI tool for something I keep fixing manually:

Linux service accounts with /bin/bash instead of /sbin/nologin.

Tweet 2/6:
userctl — 3 commands I needed:

• audit — Scan accounts, flag interactive shells
• apply -f users.yaml — Declarative provisioning
• restrict — Auto-enforce /sbin/nologin

One Bash script. Zero dependencies. Works everywhere.

Tweet 4/6:
Real use case I hit this month:

CI pipeline creating service accounts with /bin/bash "just in case."

"Just in case" becomes attack surface. /sbin/nologin is not paranoia — it is table stakes for least privilege.

---

Tweet 5/6:
Why /sbin/nologin over /bin/false?

• Logs "account not available" — audit trail
• PAM integration
• Modern standard (RHEL, Ubuntu)

Small choice. Big difference when SOC2 auditors ask for proof.
